1. What information do we collect?

In short: PulsePost works with the files and details you give it, and with basic information about the YouTube channel you connect. We only receive personal information directly if you contact us.

Information PulsePost works with on your computer

  • Video files you add to the Video Tray. PulsePost reads each file's resolution, duration, and size to check that it meets YouTube Shorts requirements.
  • Thumbnail images you choose. PulsePost reads their resolution and file size.
  • Video details you enter: title, description, tags, visibility (Public, Unlisted, or Private), and the "Made for Kids" setting.

This information stays on your computer until you choose to upload a video to YouTube. We do not receive it.

Information from your Google account

When you connect a YouTube channel, PulsePost receives an access token from Google and reads your channel's name. See section 2 for details.

Information you give us directly

If you email us or use our contact page, we receive your name, email address, and anything you include in your message.

Information collected automatically

PulsePost does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking. Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you visit. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Sensitive information

We do not process sensitive personal information.

2. YouTube API Services and Google user data

In short: PulsePost uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to your channel. It uses your Google data only for that.

PulsePost uses YouTube API Services. By using PulsePost, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

You sign in through Google's own sign-in page; PulsePost never sees your Google password. With your permission, PulsePost can:

  • Upload videos to your YouTube channel and set their thumbnails.
  • Read your channel's name, to show which channel is connected.

PulsePost's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not sell it or transfer it to anyone.

Revoking access: you can remove PulsePost's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

6. Where is your information stored, and for how long?

  • Sign-in token: after you connect YouTube, Google's sign-in library saves an access token on your computer, in the Google.Apis.Auth folder inside %APPDATA%. It stays there until you delete it or revoke access. It is never sent to us.
  • Videos and details: the Video Tray and everything you enter in the Inspector are kept in memory only while PulsePost is open. PulsePost does not copy your video files.
  • Uploaded videos belong to your YouTube channel and are kept by YouTube until you delete them in YouTube Studio.
  • Messages you send us are kept for as long as needed to answer you, and no longer than twelve (12) months after your request is resolved, unless the law requires otherwise.
